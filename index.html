Start with the frame.

Take the cladding off and a building is a load path: slab to beam, beam to column, column to caisson. I have worked both ends of it: a steel frame in the analysis model, and the caissons going into the ground at Pape Station.

Lateral drift, exaggerated so the deflected shape is visible. Let go and the frame springs back. An illustrative model, not a design.

  • Steel frame analysis and verification I re-solved our SAP2000 model of a two-bay steel frame with a direct-stiffness solver I wrote. Total load agreed within 4.7%, but the support reactions differed by up to 1.4 kN. The likeliest cause: the property table we submitted was not the model we ran.
  • Deep foundations at Pape Station One co-op term on the below-grade work for an Ontario Line station: tracking RFIs and submittals, documenting load tests, and keeping quantities and as-builts current across diaphragm walls, caissons and shoring.

Then the skin.

The envelope decides where the heat goes, and the weak points are the junctions: slab edges, balconies, parapets. At Entuitive I modelled those details in Flixo, THERM and HEAT3 for energy-code compliance. Most I modelled twice, as built and as retrofitted, so the two could be compared.

As built: slab edges, balconies and parapets run straight through the insulation, so that is where the heat leaves.

An exterior thermogram of the model on a cold night. Illustrative, not project data.

  • Envelope thermal-bridge studies A steel roof-to-wall connection and a balcony slab, each modelled from existing to continuous insulation to show whether the insulation path stays unbroken around the junction.
  • Restoration field review at Brown Beattie Inspected waterproofing and concrete repair on restoration sites, and coordinated rehabilitation projects from tender review through substantial performance.

Then the data.

A project leaves behind thousands of site photos and reports that nobody can search. I build tools that read them. They use vision models to tag what is in a photo, hybrid retrieval to find it, and language models to draft from cited sources.

Four sample records. The real tool pins every photo to a 3D model of the building, as the model here does. I built it during my Entuitive co-op, where it is under internal evaluation.

  • ProjectSearch Type what you remember seeing and get the photo, pinned to its place on a 3D model of the building. Semantic and keyword search, fused and reranked, with a confidence rating on every field.
  • Engineering retrieval and report drafting Ask a question across engineering documents and CAD metadata and get the source passages before any drafted text. Deployed on Google Cloud with Vertex AI.
  • Travers Solar: capacity and temperature First-author paper on a 465 MW plant. A residual model on top of the physics cut module-temperature error by about half (MAE 2.99 to 1.43 °C, R² 0.60 to 0.91), and automated ASTM E2848 record selection closely reproduced a manual review.
