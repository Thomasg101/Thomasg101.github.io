Residence Site Redevelopment

Civil 3D grading and site-design leadership for a student-residence concept, supported by a final drawing package and a coordinated team presentation.

01 / Context

A residence concept grounded in its site

The team developed a redevelopment concept for a student-residence site, bringing together the building proposal, circulation, grading, accessibility, vegetation, water, energy, and material considerations in one coordinated scheme.

The civil challenge was to turn a broad design concept into a coherent site drawing: the proposed building and paths needed to connect to the existing context while remaining legible enough to support the rest of the team’s work.

02 / My role

Civil 3D drawing, grading, and poster research

The team role agreement identifies me as responsible for the Civil 3D drawing and grading work, with an additional research contribution to the final poster. I used the team’s evolving proposal as the basis for the civil site plan and coordinated revisions into the final drawing package.

Responsibility boundary: the architectural rendering, drone work, LiDAR work, and InfraWorks scope are not attributed to me. They appear only as context within the team’s final board.

03 / What I did

Translated the concept into a civil site drawing

  • Developed and maintained the Civil 3D drawing used for the proposed site and grading work.
  • Coordinated building placement, site connections, accessible circulation, and the grading representation with the team’s evolving proposal.
  • Prepared the civil drawing output for the final DWG and proposed-design package.
  • Contributed site and sustainability research to the final presentation poster while preserving the distinction between individual and team work.

04 / Approach

Coordinate geometry before presentation

  1. Establish the site base. Organize the available site information and the team’s proposed footprint in one drawing environment.
  2. Resolve the civil connections. Develop grading and circulation relationships between the building, paths, accessible routes, and surrounding site.
  3. Coordinate revisions. Update the civil drawing as the interdisciplinary proposal changed, checking that shared geometry remained consistent.
  4. Package the result. Prepare the final DWG and communicate the civil contribution clearly in the team poster and design package.

05 / Outcome

A traceable team deliverable

The project concluded with a final Civil 3D drawing, a proposed-design package, and a presentation board that connected site analysis to the team’s residence concept. The role agreement and saved outputs make my individual contribution—Civil 3D, grading, and poster research—directly traceable.

The project is presented as an academic concept rather than a permitted or constructed design.
